About Us

Project Manas is a student-led initiative created to uncover the main sources of stress facing students today while sparking open conversations around mental health. In partnership with the NGO Vidya and Child, we interviewed students at the grassroots level to gather their personal stories, while simultaneously running a widespread survey to better understand what weighs on them most. The project culminates in Under the Pressure, a 4-episode podcast hosted by Jiana, which dives into our interview findings and breaks down the psychological drivers behind student stress.

why manas

Mind, named

Derived from the Sanskrit word Manas (मनस्)—meaning the mind, thought, and the inner faculty of perception and feeling—Project Manas was created because student stress in India simply isn't talked about as much as it should be. Our goal is to listen to that inner voice and elevate personal stories, offering a safe, judgment-free space where students feel heard and supported as they share their experiences.
