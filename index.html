what we stand for

Three quiet commitments

Awareness

Spreading mental health awareness by talking about day-to-day stress factors and bringing these everyday struggles into the open

Community

Partnering with a local NGO and leveraging digital platforms to ensure mental health awareness reaches every socioeconomic background and age group.

Creating a Safe Space

This project aims to provide a judgment-free, safe space for people to talk about their daily problems.
