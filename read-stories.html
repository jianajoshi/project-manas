Sarah 17 F

Grade 12

Looking at your daily life and routines, what are the main things that cause you the most stress or pressure?

Academic pressure, upcoming exams, assignments, and expectations about future studies cause me the most stress. Managing schoolwork along with personal responsibilities and maintaining good grades can sometimes feel overwhelming.

When this stress or busy routine continues for days at a time, how do you notice it affecting your body or energy levels, such as fatigue, trouble sleeping or difficulty in focusing?

When stress continues for several days, I usually feel mentally tired and find it harder to concentrate. I may also have difficulty maintaining a proper sleep schedule and feel less energetic during the day.

When you are already stressed, do you feel like you have enough time and support to handle it, or does it feel overwhelming handling it on your own?

I generally have support from my family and friends, but managing everything can still feel overwhelming at times. With schoolwork, deadlines, and personal responsibilities happening together, I sometimes feel that I need more time to handle things calmly.

When you think about a typical day, what impacts your mood and mindset the most — is it academic pressure, your own personal expectations, or social dynamics with peers?

Academic pressure and my own expectations affect my mood the most. I often worry about performing well and meeting my goals. Social situations with peers can also influence my mindset, but academics remain my main concern.

How much do your daily surroundings, like a noisy or crowded school environment, or constant smartphone notifications, affect how calm or stressed you feel throughout the day?

A noisy or crowded environment can make it harder for me to concentrate and feel relaxed. Constant notifications can also be distracting, especially while studying, so I prefer having a quiet environment with fewer interruptions.
