Student voices

Poonit Singh 18 M

Grade 12

Question

Looking at your daily life and routines, what are the main things that cause you the most stress or pressure?

Answer

Study cycle, preparation for exams.

Question

When this stress or busy routine continues for days at a time, how do you notice it affecting your body or energy levels, such as fatigue, trouble sleeping or difficulty in focusing?

Answer

Lower energy throughout the day.

Question

When you are already stressed, do you feel like you have enough time and support to handle it, or does it feel overwhelming handling it on your own?

Answer

It feels overwhelming to handle it on my own.

Question

When you think about a typical day, what impacts your mood and mindset the most — is it academic pressure, your own personal expectations, or social dynamics with peers?

Answer

My own personal expectations.

Question

How much do your daily surroundings, like a noisy or crowded school environment, or constant smartphone notifications, affect how calm or stressed you feel throughout the day?

Answer

Talking to my friends generally calms me down. It makes me forget about the different things I have to constantly worry about.

Manavi Sharma

Grade 10, 15 years

Manavi opens up about the constant heavy-headed feeling from tight homework deadlines, carrying the mindset that she has to handle all the academic pressure on her own.
