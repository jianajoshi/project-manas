Stories

Read and listen to stories from students across India sharing their honest experiences with academic pressure, family expectations, and mental health Beyond the statistics and report cards are real voices trying to balance their daily lives while looking for a safe space to be heard.
These unfiltered accounts highlight the quiet struggles students face every day—from late-night studying to the pressure of keeping up.
Explore their journeys, hear their perspectives, and discover why creating an open conversation around student stress matters now more than ever.

Kanak

Grade 10, 15 years

Kanak shares how hard it is to balance school with housework, mostly keeping her stress to herself and writing in her diary because she feels like no one is really listening.

Ankit

Grade 10, 17 years

Ankit shares how constant deadlines mess with his sleep and mood, relying on music and talks with his sister to deal with family expectations.

Shagun

Grade 10, 14 years

Shagun shares how continuous assignments give her headaches, making it tough to stay focused when dealing with phone notifications and family comparisons.

Dev

Grade 10, 16 years

Dev talks about feeling worn out by upcoming tests and homework, pushing through the fatigue because he feels a strong responsibility to make his family proud.

Arjun

Grade 10, 16 years

Arjun talks about getting stuck during self-study and losing focus, dealing with the pressure of university competition by just stepping away from his work when it gets too much.

Manavi Sharma

Grade 10, 15 years

Manavi opens up about the constant heavy-headed feeling from tight homework deadlines, carrying the mindset that she has to handle all the academic pressure on her own.
